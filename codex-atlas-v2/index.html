£30m Hotel Restoration

South Croydon, Greater London

Sector: HospitalityPotential scope: Heating & mechanical

01 / Project overview

Restoration of an existing hotel, including guest accommodation and shared facilities, with replacement of an ageing heating system. The £30m is the assumed overall project budget — not the value of an HVAC package.

02 / Potential HVAC scope

  • Heating plant: proposed heat source, plant replacement, distribution.
  • Hot water: demand, storage, any change to guest-room provision.
  • Ventilation: whether kitchens, bathrooms and shared areas need upgrades.
  • Controls & commissioning: integration and responsibility for handover.

Questions

What contractors usually ask.

Where do the projects come from?

They start as public project signals — a planning application, a project announcement or a reported development. Each one is then researched and qualified before it reaches you.

Is a brief a verified live lead?

No brief claims more than the evidence supports. A researched brief separates source-backed findings, commercial interpretation and open questions, with source links and dates for the underlying claims.

What do I do with a brief?

Every brief ends with a practical next action — who to approach and what to confirm — so you can decide quickly whether it deserves estimating time.

What happens after the four weeks?

You decide. Continue only if the intelligence has been useful. There is no long-term commitment.

Who is behind Atlas?

Atlas is founder-led. You work directly with the person reviewing the research and can explain what makes an opportunity useful to you.
