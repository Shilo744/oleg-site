Atlas opportunity brief / illustrative sample

£30m Hotel Restoration

South Croydon, Greater London.
A worked example of turning a project signal into a focused commercial investigation.

Illustrative sample — not a live lead.
The project, budget and scope below are a fictional demonstration. This is not a report on a named hotel or real project. No tender, contractor appointment or procurement availability has been verified.

01Project overview

Consider a £30m restoration of an existing hotel in South Croydon. In this scenario, refurbishment includes guest accommodation and shared facilities, with replacement of an ageing heating system.

The £30m figure represents the assumed overall project budget, not the value of an HVAC package. A project of this kind could warrant investigation by a commercial HVAC contractor with refurbishment experience.

02Potential HVAC scope

  • Heating plant: investigate the proposed heat source, plant replacement and distribution requirements.
  • Hot water: clarify demand, storage and any changes to guest-room provision.
  • Ventilation: establish whether kitchens, bathrooms and shared areas require upgrades.
  • Controls and commissioning: check integration requirements and responsibility for testing and handover.

These are investigation prompts, not confirmed specifications. A new heating system alone would not establish the scope or availability of a subcontract package.

04What still needs confirming

Before committing estimating time, establish the project programme, the actual mechanical scope, whether the package is already placed, and who controls the shortlist. Confirm any trading, insurance or accreditation requirements with the procuring organisation.

05Recommended next action

Start with procurement verification. Identify the appointed main contractor through reliable project documentation, then ask its procurement team whether mechanical services are still being procured and how to register interest.

If the package remains available and fits your capacity, request scope and programme information before preparing a proposal. If it is already placed, record that outcome and redirect your effort.
